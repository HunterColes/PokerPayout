Free, no ads, offline. No internet permission, no accounts, no tracking.

Everything for a home poker night: a tournament clock, payouts that add up to the cent, a bank of who paid what, and tools for the table.

TOURNAMENT
• Set up the night once: players, buy-in, bounty, rebuys and late entry (each with a cutoff), add-ons, food and an optional house rake. Save it as a preset, or start from a ready-made night
• Blinds from four numbers: game length, level length, starting stack and smallest chip. Every level is a multiple of your smallest chip, and a setup that can't work says why and offers the nearest fix
• A blind clock with big digits, the next level, ±1 minute nudges, breaks with a note and a big-blind ante. It keeps time through sleep, restarts and reboots
• The running clock in the notifications and on the lock screen, with a chime, a vibration or a flash for each new level
• Color-ups on breaks, and overtime levels if the game runs long
• Turn the phone sideways for a full-screen table view, with knockouts right from the clock
• The big moments on the clock: the bubble, in the money, the final table, heads-up and the champion

THE BANK AND PAYOUTS
• Track buy-ins, rebuys, add-ons, knockouts, bounties and who has been paid, with Undo
• Pick tonight's players from the people you've played with
• Late arrivals and re-entries, each with its own buy-in and stack
• Bounties three ways: standard, progressive (half now, half onto the winner's own bounty) or mystery envelopes
• Top-heavy, standard and flat payouts, or your own weights. The places always add up to the prize pool
• Settle up at the end: the fewest payments so everyone is square, with a tick for each
• Share the payouts or the settle-up as text

TOOLS
• Chip set: stacks from the chips you own, and a color-up plan
• Seat draw: random seats and the high card for the button
• Odds for two to ten hands, and run it out card by card
• Side pots when someone is all in
• Hand ranks
• Split the prizes: a deal by ICM or chip chop
• History: saved nights, season points and a CSV export
• Cue sounds, and music from your phone that plays with the clock
• Your currency: $, €, £, ₹, ¥, kr and more, or none
• Backup: everything in one file you keep
